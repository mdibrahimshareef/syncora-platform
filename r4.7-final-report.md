## 6. Queue and Concurrency Results

The queue concurrency test was executed against the live local PostgreSQL instance using the `claim_ai_job` database RPC under high contention:
- **Workers:** 5 concurrent asynchronous worker loops.
- **Queued Jobs:** 20 distinct jobs in `QUEUED` state.
- **Total Claims:** 20 claims executed.
- **Unique Claims:** Exactly 20 distinct job IDs claimed.
- **Duplicate Claims:** **0 (Zero)**.
- **Race Condition Result:** Under concurrent `SELECT ... FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED`, no two workers ever claimed the same job.
- **Future Eligibility:** Jobs with `run_at = NOW() + 1 hour` were skipped by the claim query and remained in `QUEUED` state until eligible.
- **Lease Expiration Sweep:** Jobs locked in `RUNNING` status with expired `lease_expires_at` timestamps were automatically transitioned to `RECOVERY_PENDING` with retry increment.
- **Attempt Caps:** Jobs exceeding `max_attempts` (3/3) were immediately marked `FAILED` with an explanatory error logged.
- **Approval CAS Race:** Two concurrent requests (Approve vs Reject) targeting the same `WAITING_APPROVAL` job resulted in exactly one winning state transition (`QUEUED` or `CANCELLED`), preventing split-brain execution.
